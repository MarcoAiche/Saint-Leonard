1.2 Core Game Loop:
1. Customize Redneck character in live 3D Character Creator (Sliders, Randomise, Save) [V5 00:05-00:08].
2. Start at Camp Plaza (Hunting Lodge, Butcher Food Truck, Weapon Shack, Wildlife Field Office, RV/Trailers) [V1, V6, V8, V9].
3. Drive Pickup Truck (with cargo bed) or Green ATV out into Forest, River, Swamp, or Snow Mountain biomes [V2, V6, V7].
4. Hunt aggressive/mutant alien-infected animals (Boar, Deer/Stag, Rabbit -> Mutant Rabbit, Bear, Bigfoot Boss) using progressive arsenal (Pistol, Shotgun, Bolt-Action, AK-47, LMG, Sniper, Bazooka, Alien Blaster, Melee Punch) [V1-V9].
5. Load dead ('X'-eyed) animal carcasses into Pickup cargo bed (or carry by hand/antlers) and haul back to Camp [V3, V6].
6. Sell carcasses directly to Butcher NPC ("GASTON") for guaranteed cash ($300 for Deer) OR feed carcasses into "LUCKY BUCK" Slot Machine inside Hunting Lodge to gamble up to 10x pot [V1-V8].
7. Buy upgraded weapons, ammo, scopes, and alien tech at the Weapon Shack, drink Moonshine with co-op buddies for chaotic drunk effects, and unlock new areas / fight Bigfoot & Alien Bosses [V1, V2, V4, V7, V9].
